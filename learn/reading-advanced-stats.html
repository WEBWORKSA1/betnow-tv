Reading Advanced Stats: xG, EPA, Net Rating and Corsi

By the BetNow.tv Analytics Desk · 7 min read · Updated 2026-11-16

Advanced stats exist because traditional box scores mix skill with luck. Each sport has developed metrics that isolate the repeatable parts of performance. Here are the essentials.

Soccer: expected goals (xG)

xG assigns each shot a probability of scoring based on historical shots with similar location, angle, body part and assist type. A penalty is roughly 0.76 xG; a long-range strike might be 0.03. Summing a team's xG shows the quality of chances it created, regardless of finishing luck. Over a season, xG difference predicts future results better than actual goal difference.

NFL: expected points added (EPA)

Every down-distance-field position has an expected point value. EPA measures how much a play changed it. A 15-yard completion on 3rd-and-10 adds a lot; a 3-yard run on 3rd-and-10 subtracts. EPA per play is the standard efficiency metric for offences and quarterbacks, often paired with success rate.

NBA: net rating and true shooting

  • Offensive / defensive rating: points scored or allowed per 100 possessions, removing pace.
  • Net rating: the difference — the best single number for team quality.
  • True shooting %: scoring efficiency including threes and free throws.

NHL: Corsi, Fenwick and xGF%

Corsi counts all shot attempts for minus against while a team or player is on ice; Fenwick excludes blocked shots. Expected goals for percentage (xGF%) weights attempts by quality. These possession proxies are far more stable than goals in a low-scoring sport.

MLB: wOBA, FIP and run differential

wOBA weights each offensive outcome by its run value; FIP judges pitchers on strikeouts, walks and home runs — outcomes they control most. Pythagorean expectation turns run differential into an expected win percentage.

When to trust them

Advanced metrics still need sample size, and they have blind spots (defensive positioning, game state). Use them to ask better questions, not to end debates. Our predictions and tools are built on these ideas.
