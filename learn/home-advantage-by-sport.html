Home Advantage: How Much Is It Worth in Each Sport?

By the BetNow.tv Analytics Desk · 5 min read · Updated 2026-11-16

Home advantage is one of the most studied effects in sport. It is real, it differs by sport, and it has been shrinking for decades. Here is what the evidence shows and how models use it.

Approximate home win rates

SportTypical home win share (historical)
Soccer (top European leagues)~45% wins, ~26% draws
NBA~57–60%
NFL~55–57%
NHL~54–55%
MLB~53–54%

Ranges summarise long-run historical figures widely reported in sports-analytics literature; exact values vary by season and league.

Where does it come from?

  • Officiating bias: studies of soccer referees suggest crowd noise nudges marginal decisions (fouls, added time).
  • Travel and fatigue: especially in the NBA and NHL with back-to-back games and time-zone changes.
  • Familiarity: MLB parks differ physically; altitude matters in Denver for several sports.

The empty-stadium natural experiment

When games were played without fans in 2020, several studies of European soccer found home advantage dropped noticeably, with referee foul and card counts becoming more balanced — strong evidence that crowds drive a meaningful share of the effect.

How models use it

Elo models add a rating bonus to the home side; Poisson models use separate home and away scoring rates. Our predictions include a sport-specific home factor. Good practice is to re-estimate it every season, because the long-term trend has been a gradual decline.
