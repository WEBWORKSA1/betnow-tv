Free Pick'em Contest Strategy: How to Win More Often

By the BetNow.tv Analytics Desk · 5 min read · Updated 2026-11-16

Free Pick'em contests reward getting results right, but winning a contest is different from maximising correct picks. Strategy depends on scoring rules and how many people you compete against.

Small pools: maximise accuracy

With a handful of rivals, the player with the most correct picks usually wins. Pick the side with the higher probability in every game — our model probabilities are a good starting point.

Large pools: differentiate

With thousands of entrants, many people pick the same favourites, so matching the crowd guarantees you will tie with hundreds. To finish first you need correct picks others missed. The smart approach is selective contrarianism: choose a few close games (45–55% on the model) where the public heavily favours one side, and take the other.

Confidence-point formats

When you rank games by confidence, assign your highest points to the largest probability gaps, not your favourite team. Avoid putting top points on coin flips.

Track your calibration

Write down a probability for each pick. After a season, check how often your 60% picks came true. This is the fastest way to improve — and it is how professional forecasters train.
