Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-16.

This policy explains what information BetNow.tv ("we") collects and how it is used.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (newsletter, contest entry, pledge, application or contact), we receive the details you enter, such as name, email and message. Form submissions are delivered through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit) to our private inbox. We use this data only to respond, deliver the newsletter, run contests or process your request. We do not sell personal data.

Information stored on your device

Your picks, trivia streak, theme and cookie choice are stored in your browser's local storage and never leave your device unless you submit a form.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more in How Google uses data from partner sites. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalised ads are served.

Embedded content

Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and load only after you click play. Fonts are served by Google Fonts. These providers may collect technical data under their own policies.

Children

BetNow.tv is intended for adults 18+. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA) via the contact form (topic: Privacy request). Newsletter emails include an unsubscribe option.

Retention & security

We keep form data only as long as needed for its purpose, then delete it. The site is served over HTTPS.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
