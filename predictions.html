Methodology

Soccer forecasts use a Poisson goals model. Each club's attack and defence strength comes from current-season goals scored and conceded per game relative to the league average, with a home-advantage factor. We compute the full scoreline grid and sum home-win, draw and away-win probabilities. How Poisson models work →

NFL, NBA, NHL and MLB forecasts use a rating model derived from current win percentage and point differential, converted to win probability with a logistic (Elo-style) curve plus a sport-specific home edge. How Elo works →

Fair odds are simply 1 ÷ probability — the price at which the forecast would be break-even. They are shown for education only.

Limitations

  • Early-season tables are noisy; probabilities regress toward 50/50 when few games are played.
  • Models do not see late injury news, rotation or weather.
  • Ties in US sports are rare and treated as a split.
