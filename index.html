Frequently asked questions

Is BetNow.tv a betting site?

No. BetNow.tv is a free sports information, education and entertainment site. We never accept wagers, never process bets and never link to sportsbooks or gambling operators.

Where do the predictions come from?

Our models use public fixture and league-table data, refreshed automatically every day. Soccer uses a Poisson goals model; US sports use a rating-based model with home advantage. Methodology is published on the Predictions page.

Are the contests really free?

Yes. Every contest is free to enter, no purchase necessary. Prizes are funded by sponsors and community supporters. Entrants must be 18+ and contests are void where prohibited.

How do I advertise or sponsor?

Visit Advertise & Sponsor and send the form, or use the inquiry link at the top of every page.

How is the site funded?

Through display advertising, sponsorships and voluntary community support. Supporters fund data feeds, prizes, creators and hiring.
