# BetNow.tv — Idea selection & competitive benchmark

## The winning idea
**Free sports-prediction + odds-education + highlight-video hub with free-to-play prize contests.** No real-money wagering, no sportsbook links.

Why this idea and not a betting/affiliate site:

| Option | AdSense approval | Ad demand | Revenue ceiling | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sportsbook affiliate / odds comparison | Risky | **Restricted serving** (Google Publisher Restrictions: online gambling) | High per-lead, but needs licences and geo-compliance | ❌ conflicts with the AdSense-first goal |
| Real-money betting | Not possible | — | — | ❌ |
| **Free predictions + odds-math education + videos + free contests** | Strong (educational gambling content can show ads unrestricted) | Full sports/tech/finance advertiser demand | AdSense + sponsorships + newsletter + B2B leads + donations | ✅ chosen |

The exact-match "bet now" keyword intent is captured with "predictions", "odds explained", "pick'em" and "who will win" content, which converts that traffic into ad impressions and email leads.

## Revenue stack (in order of expected contribution)
1. **AdSense auto ads** on high-pageview pages (predictions refresh daily, so visitors come back daily)
2. **Contest & newsletter sponsorships** (non-gambling brands: apparel, streaming, fantasy free-to-play, tech)
3. **Lead generation**: Edge Report email list (consumer), B2B partner/advertiser leads, custom-model requests
4. **Donations / supporter tiers**
5. **Domain/website acquisition inquiries** (top bar on every page)

## Compliance decisions
- No gambling-operator links or ads sold; 18+ notices; responsible-gaming page with helplines
- Free contests only, "no purchase necessary" rules, donation ≠ entry advantage, Quebec skill-question note
- Privacy policy with the Google advertising-cookie disclosures AdSense requires; cookie banner; ads.txt
- Non-affiliation + trademark disclosure against any "BetNow" brand; no league logos; YouTube embeds only via the official player
