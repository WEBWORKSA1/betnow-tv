About BetNow.tv

An independent, free sports-analytics publication.

BetNow.tv exists to make sports probability understandable. Fans see numbers everywhere — prices, power rankings, win probabilities — but rarely get an honest explanation of what they mean. We publish transparent model forecasts, free calculators and plain-English guides so anyone can think like an analyst.

What we believe

  • Transparency: methods are published; limitations are stated.
  • No wagering: we never take bets or promote gambling operators. Our contests are free.
  • Education first: every tool links to a guide explaining the math.
  • Fan-funded independence: advertising, sponsorship and supporters fund the site.

How the site stays fresh

Fixtures, predictions and headlines are refreshed automatically several times a day from public data sources. Our editorial team writes and reviews all guides.

Editorial standards

Guides are written by the BetNow.tv Analytics Desk and reviewed for accuracy. Spot an error? Tell us — corrections are made promptly.
