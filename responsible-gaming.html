Responsible Gaming

Last updated 2026-11-16.

18+BetNow.tv is a free, no-wagering site. We publish predictions and probability education because understanding probability helps people make informed, healthier decisions. If you gamble elsewhere, these principles and resources matter.

Warning signs

  • Spending more time or money than intended
  • Chasing losses or feeling a result is "due"
  • Borrowing money or hiding activity from family
  • Feeling anxious or irritable when not gambling
  • Gambling to escape problems or low mood

Healthy habits

  • Treat any gambling as entertainment spending, never income.
  • Set time and money limits in advance and keep them.
  • Never gamble while upset, tired or under the influence.
  • Remember the maths: market margins mean most people lose over time (see margin explained).

Free, confidential help

  • USA: 1-800-GAMBLER · National Council on Problem Gambling
  • Canada: ConnexOntario 1-866-531-2600 · Responsible Gambling Council
  • UK: BeGambleAware · National Gambling Helpline 0808 8020 133 · GAMSTOP self-exclusion
  • Australia: Gambling Help Online 1800 858 858
  • International: Gamblers Anonymous

Blocking tools

Free tools such as Gamban, BetBlocker and device-level parental controls can block gambling sites and apps.
